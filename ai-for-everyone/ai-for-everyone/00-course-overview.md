# AI for Everyone — Course Overview & Syllabus

> **Instructor**: Andrew Ng
> **Platform**: Coursera / DeepLearning.AI
> **Audience**: Non-technical learners (leaders, professionals, curious minds)
> **Duration**: ~6h 54m · **35 video lessons** · 4 weeks
> These are original structured study notes summarizing each lesson's key teaching points (not verbatim transcripts).

## About the Course
AI for Everyone is a non-technical introduction to artificial intelligence. It teaches how to **navigate the rise of AI** — understanding what AI is, how to build and choose AI projects, how to make an organization good at AI, and how to think about AI's impact on society. No coding or math background required.

## Key Themes
- **Supervised learning as A→B** is the core engine of today's AI value.
- **Data** is the strategic asset; quality and labeling beat raw volume.
- **Project selection** = technical feasibility × business value.
- The **AI Transformation Playbook** turns an organization into an "AI-first" company.
- **Responsible AI** = addressing bias, security, and adverse uses while staying realistic.
