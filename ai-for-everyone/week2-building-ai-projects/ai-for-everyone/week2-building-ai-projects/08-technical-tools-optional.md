# Week 2 — Technical Tools for AI Teams (optional)

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **2 — Building AI Projects** · Lesson: **Technical tools for AI teams** · *optional*
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
An optional, high-level tour of the software stack AI teams use — so non-technical leaders recognize the pieces without needing to master them.

## Key Points
- **Languages & frameworks**:
  - **Python** is the dominant language in ML/AI.
  - Frameworks like **TensorFlow / PyTorch** are used to build and train neural networks.
- **Compute**: training large models often uses **GPUs** and cloud platforms (e.g., cloud providers offering training and inference services).
- **Pre-built services**: many tasks (speech-to-text, image recognition, translation) are available as **cloud APIs**, so teams often don't build from scratch.
- **Off-the-shelf vs. custom**: teams choose between ready-made APIs and custom models depending on data advantage and differentiation needs.
- **Practical message for leaders**: you don't need to know the tools' details — but you should know enough to gauge **build vs. buy** and to staff appropriately.

## Takeaways
- Python + frameworks (TensorFlow/PyTorch) + GPU/cloud compute are the common stack.
- Cloud APIs cover many standard tasks; custom models matter mainly when you have a data edge.
- Leaders just need enough awareness to scope build-vs-buy and resourcing.
