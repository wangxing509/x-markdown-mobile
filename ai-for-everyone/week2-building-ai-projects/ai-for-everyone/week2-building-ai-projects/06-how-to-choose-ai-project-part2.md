# Week 2 — How to Choose an AI Project (Part 2)

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **2 — Building AI Projects** · Lesson: **How to choose an AI project (Part 2)**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
Part 2 adds practical filters for shortlisting projects: the "buy vs. build" decision, ROI/bias trade-offs, and the value of **piloting** before full commitment.

## Key Points
- **Due diligence across three dimensions** before committing to a project:
  - **Technical** — can data/ML actually solve the A→B task?
  - **Business** — is there clear ROI?
  - **Ethical** — are there biases or adverse-use risks you'd be accountable for?
- **Buy vs. build**: many AI capabilities can be purchased off-the-shelf (cloud APIs for vision, speech, language). Only build when the capability is a genuine differentiator and you have the data to make it better than what's on the market.
- **Proof-of-concept / pilot first**: run a small, bounded pilot or proof-of-concept to validate feasibility and value before committing a large budget. This de-risks the choice and builds internal buy-in.
- **Bias toward value**: prefer projects where even a modest accuracy improvement translates into large business impact (e.g., high-volume, high-margin workflows).
- **Look for projects that compound**: choose projects where the data you collect feeds future models (ties back to the data flywheel).
- **Start small, then expand**: the flywheel starts with one focused success that produces data, then compounds into more projects.
- **Build an AI portfolio**: hold a **mix of near-term quick wins and longer-term strategic bets**, rather than betting everything on a single "cool" AI project.
- **Multi-function collaboration**: the best project selection often comes from pairing domain experts (who know the business) with AI talent (who know what's feasible).

## Takeaways
- Prefer **buy over build** unless you have a defensible data advantage.
- Pick projects with high value per point of accuracy and that build data moats.
- **Pilot small first** to validate before scaling investment.
