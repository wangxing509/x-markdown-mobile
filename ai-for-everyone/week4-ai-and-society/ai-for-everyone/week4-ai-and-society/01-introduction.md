# Week 4 — AI & Society: Introduction

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **4 — AI & Society** · Lesson: **Introduction**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
The final week steps back from building AI to ask how AI affects people and society — covering the honest limitations, bias, security, adverse uses, and the economic/social impacts on economies and jobs.

## Key Points
- **Theme**: AI is not only a technical or business matter; it has profound social consequences that leaders and citizens should understand and navigate.
- **Topics covered this week**:
  - A **realistic view** of AI's actual capabilities and limitations (avoiding both hype and doom).
  - How AI systems can be **biased or discriminatory**, and how to reduce such bias.
  - **Adversarial attacks** and **adverse uses** of AI.
  - AI's impact on **developing economies**.
  - AI's impact on **jobs** and what that means for careers and society.
- **Goal**: after this week, learners should be able to think clearly and lead others through the societal changes AI brings.
- **Balanced stance**: Ng's message is consistently measured — AI creates real value and real risks, and neither panic nor dismissal is warranted.

## Takeaways
- AI's social dimension matters as much as its technical one.
- The week's lens: **realistic expectations + responsible use + forward-looking adaptation**.
- Equip yourself to reason about bias, security, and jobs with evidence, not fear.
