# Week 4 — A Realistic View of AI

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **4 — AI & Society** · Lesson: **A realistic view of AI**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
The right mindset for AI is the **"Goldilocks rule"**: avoid the two extreme views — that AI is unstoppable and will take over (over-optimism/doom), and that AI is overhyped and worthless (over-pessimism/"AI winter").

## Key Points
- **Two unhelpful extremes**:
  - **Over-optimism**: believing killer robots / AGI are imminent, fueling misplaced fear.
  - **Over-pessimism**: concluding AI is all hype and ignoring the real value it already creates (the "AI winter" failure mode).
- **The Goldilocks rule**: the accurate picture is in between — **AI is a powerful but narrow tool** that creates genuine value today while remaining far from general intelligence.
- **Evidence of real value**: AI already powers search, ads, recommendations, translation, speech, image understanding, and is expanding into healthcare, manufacturing, and other sectors.
- **Evidence against doomsday**: current AI is ANI (narrow) plus growing generative capabilities; AGI remains distant, requiring many breakthroughs.
- **Implication for leaders and citizens**: evaluate AI claims critically; invest and adopt where value is real, but don't overreact to speculative fears.

## Takeaways
- Hold a **balanced, evidence-based** view of AI.
- Value is real; existential risk in the near term is not.
- Make decisions based on measured expectations, not hype or panic.
