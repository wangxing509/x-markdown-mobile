# Week 4 — AI and Jobs

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **4 — AI & Society** · Lesson: **AI and jobs**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
AI will **displace some jobs and create others**, with the biggest impact on **routine, repetitive work**. The most resilient career strategy is to **layer AI onto your existing expertise** rather than fight it.

## Key Points
- **What gets automated first**: **routine, repetitive tasks** — the kind AI (and software before it) automates most easily. People whose work is largely routine face the greatest displacement risk.
- **What's harder to automate**: work requiring **judgment, creativity, human interaction, and rich context** — though generative AI is expanding what's automatable, so even "white collar" tasks are changing.
- **Scale of the shift**: McKinsey projections cited in the course suggest roughly **400–800 million jobs displaced** and **555–890 million jobs created** globally by 2030 — i.e., massive **churn**, not a simple net loss. The picture is one of *transformation*, not "all jobs disappear."
- **Policy ideas**: a **conditional basic income** that incentivizes learning; building a **lifelong learning society**; and further political/economic solutions.
- **The recommended career approach**:
  - **Don't try to out-compete AI** at the routine work it does well.
  - **Become an AI-savvy expert in your field** — rather than switching fields, learn to **apply AI on top of your existing expertise**, making yourself more uniquely qualified for valuable work.
  - **Reskill proactively**; invest in learning the AI tools relevant to your profession.
- **For leaders and policymakers**: support reskilling and education to help people adapt, rather than resisting automation.

## Takeaways
- Routine work is most at risk; **AI + human expertise** is the resilient combination.
- Jobs will be **displaced and created** — the skill is adapting.
- Proactive **reskilling and AI literacy** are the best insurance for careers.
