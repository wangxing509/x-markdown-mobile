# Week 1 — What Makes an AI Company?

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **1 — What Is AI** · Lesson: **What makes an AI company?**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
Being an "AI company" is not about using AI tools — it's about making **strategic, systematic use of data** as a core asset, and building the data flywheel that powers continuous improvement.

## Key Points
- **The Internet-company analogy**: a shopping mall with a website is NOT an Internet company. An Internet company does what the internet enables *really well* — pervasive **A/B testing**, short iteration times (ship weekly/daily), and pushing decisions down from the CEO to engineers/product managers. Likewise, merely "using a few neural nets" does not make you an AI company.
- **AI-first companies do what AI enables really well**:
  - **Strategic data acquisition** — e.g., launching a product that doesn't monetize directly, solely to acquire data used to make money elsewhere.
  - **Unified data warehouse** — instead of 50 databases under 50 VPs, bring data together so teams can "connect the dots" (subject to privacy rules like GDPR).
  - **Spotting automation opportunities** — inserting supervised-learning A→B mappings to automate tasks.
  - **New roles and structures** — e.g., Machine Learning Engineer (MLE) and AI-first team setups.
- **The "data flywheel" / virtuous cycle**: a good product → attracts users → generates data → improves the model → better product → more users → more data. This compounding loop is how leaders stay ahead.
- **Strategic questions a company should ask**:
  - How do we systematically acquire data?
  - How do we use data to improve our products continuously?
  - Are data decisions centralized and deliberate, or ad hoc?

## Takeaways
- An AI company **systematically generates and exploits data** as a strategic asset.
- The **data flywheel** (product → data → better model → better product) is the core engine of competitive advantage.
- Any organization can begin moving toward being "AI-first" by deliberately designing for data capture.
