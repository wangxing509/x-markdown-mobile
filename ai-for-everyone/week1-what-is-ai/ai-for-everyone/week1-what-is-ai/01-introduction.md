# Week 1 — What Is AI: Introduction

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **1 — What Is AI** · Lesson: **Introduction**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
This lesson frames the whole course: AI is creating enormous value today and will reshape nearly every industry. The goal is to cut through hype, give a realistic view of what AI can and cannot do, and prepare learners to make practical use of AI in their own work or organization.

## Key Points
- **Scale of economic impact**: Analysts (e.g., McKinsey Global Institute studies) project AI could add roughly **$13–22 trillion** of value per year. A meaningful slice of that comes from **generative AI**, but the larger share is expected from more mature techniques such as **supervised learning**.
- **Most value lies outside software**: The biggest future gains are in retail, travel, transportation, automotive, materials, manufacturing, and other "traditional" sectors — not just the tech industry.
- **The three "AIs" that get conflated**:
  - **Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)** — single-purpose AI (smart speakers, web search, self-driving, factory/agriculture AI). These are "one-trick ponies," but extremely valuable when aimed at the right task. This is where most current value is.
  - **Generative AI** (e.g., ChatGPT, Bard) — more general-purpose; can be a copy editor, brainstorming partner, summarizer, and more.
  - **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)** — hypothetical AI matching/exceeding human intellectual ability across all tasks. Still very far away; likely requires multiple breakthroughs and possibly decades (or longer). No need for undue anxiety about it today.

## Takeaways
- ANI and generative AI are advancing quickly and delivering real value **today**.
- Conflating rapid ANI/generative progress with "AGI is near" leads to overblown fears (e.g., "evil sentient robots").
- This week establishes the vocabulary and the honest capability picture the rest of the course builds on.
