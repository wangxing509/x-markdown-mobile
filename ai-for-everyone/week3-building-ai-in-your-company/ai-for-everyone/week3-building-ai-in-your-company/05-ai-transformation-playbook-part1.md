# Week 3 — AI Transformation Playbook (Part 1)

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **3 — Building AI in Your Company** · Lesson: **AI Transformation Playbook (Part 1)**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
Ng's **AI Transformation Playbook** is a five-step framework for making a company genuinely good at AI — treating it as an organizational transformation, not a one-off technology rollout. It draws on his experience leading AI at Google Brain and Baidu.

## Key Points
- **The 5 steps (overview)**:
  1. **Execute pilot projects** to gain momentum.
  2. **Build an in-house AI team**.
  3. **Provide broad AI training**.
  4. **Develop an AI strategy**.
  5. **Develop internal & external communications**.
- **Step 1 — Pilot projects** (focus of Part 1):
  - Choose **2–3 small, tractable, high-value projects** that are technically feasible — aim for early wins to build internal credibility, not a single moonshot.
  - The goals are to build **momentum, buy-in, and internal skills** — not to transform the whole company at once.
  - Successful pilots create a proof point that convinces leadership and other teams.
- **Step 2 — Build an in-house AI team**: create a **centralized AI group** (a "Center of Excellence") that can support business units, including both technical and product/management talent.
- **Why pilots first**: it's easier and lower-risk to demonstrate value on a small project than to justify a sweeping AI vision without evidence.
- **Two mindsets to avoid**: over-committing to an ambitious but vague AI strategy before any wins, or treating pilots as one-off experiments with no path to scaling.

## Takeaways
- The playbook has 5 sequential steps; **pilot projects come first**.
- Pilots build momentum and capability, de-risking the larger transformation.
- Sequence matters: win early, then invest, then scale.
