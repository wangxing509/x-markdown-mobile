# Week 3 — AI Transformation Playbook (Part 2)

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **3 — Building AI in Your Company** · Lesson: **AI Transformation Playbook (Part 2)**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
Part 2 covers the remaining steps of the playbook: building the in-house AI team, broad AI training, developing the AI strategy, and aligning communications.

## Key Points
- **Step 2 — Build an in-house AI team**:
  - Create a central **AI group** (e.g., a "Center of Excellence") and/or embed AI people into business units.
  - A central team builds reusable infrastructure and standards; distributed teams embed AI where the business problems are.
- **Step 3 — Provide broad AI training**:
  - Train **executives** (to understand and champion AI), **business-unit leaders** (to identify projects), and **engineers** (to build). All levels need AI literacy.
- **Step 4 — Develop an AI strategy**:
  - Decide where AI gives your company a **durable competitive advantage** — often via proprietary data or an AI flywheel.
  - Align the AI portfolio with business goals, not fads.
- **Step 5 — Develop internal & external communications**:
  - **Internal**: honest, realistic messaging that builds support and manages expectations (avoid hype that fuels over-promise).
  - **External**: a credible AI story for customers, investors, and talent.
- **Recurring theme**: transformation is systemic — strategy, talent, data, and culture must move together.

## Takeaways
- Build the AI team deliberately (central + distributed), train all levels, and define a data-driven strategy.
- Communications keep the transformation credible and supported internally and externally.
- The whole playbook works only when the pieces reinforce each other.
