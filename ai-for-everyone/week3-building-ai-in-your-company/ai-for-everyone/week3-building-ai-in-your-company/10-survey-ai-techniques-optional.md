# Week 3 — Survey of Major AI Techniques (optional)

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **3 — Building AI in Your Company** · Lesson: **Survey of major AI techniques** · *optional*
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
A high-level map of the main AI technique families, so non-technical leaders can place the terms they hear (supervised vs. unsupervised vs. reinforcement learning) in context.

## Key Points
- **Supervised learning** — learn A→B from labeled examples. The workhorse of most economic value (ads, search, recommendation, vision, speech, translation).
- **Unsupervised learning** — find structure in unlabeled data (clustering, anomaly detection, dimensionality reduction). Useful for grouping customers or spotting outliers.
- **Reinforcement learning** — an agent learns by taking actions and receiving **rewards/penalties** (trial and error). Used in robotics, games, and some sequential-decision problems.
- **Transfer learning** — take a model trained on one large task and adapt it to a related task with less data. Widely used with large pre-trained models.
- **Generative models / GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks)** — produce new data (images, audio, text) rather than just classify; the basis of much generative-AI content.
- **Which to use**: match the technique to the task — classification/prediction → supervised; grouping/anomaly → unsupervised; sequential decisions → reinforcement; generating content → generative models.

## Takeaways
- Know the families: **supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement, transfer, generative**.
- Most business value today comes from supervised learning.
- Picking the right technique is a technical call, but leaders should know the categories exist and what each is for.
