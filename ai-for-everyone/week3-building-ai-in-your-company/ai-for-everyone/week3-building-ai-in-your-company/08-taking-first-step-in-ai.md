# Week 3 — Taking Your First Step in AI

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **3 — Building AI in Your Company** · Lesson: **Taking your first step in AI**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
The most important action is to **start with a real pilot** — pick one feasible, valuable project and ship it — rather than continue planning or waiting for perfect conditions.

## Key Points
- **Doing is better than planning**: a successful pilot generates data, skills, and momentum that no amount of strategy discussion can produce.
- **Recommended learning path**: first get a high-level understanding (this course), then deepen technical skills via Ng's *Machine Learning* and *Deep Learning Specialization* on Coursera.
- **How to pick your first pilot**:
  - Small enough to finish quickly.
  - Clearly **technically feasible** (a clear A→B task) with data you can access.
  - **Valuable** enough that success is visible and convincing.
- **Form a small dedicated team** and give it a concrete success metric.
- **Expect to learn and iterate**; the first pilot is as much about learning how your organization works with AI as about the technology.
- **Learn by doing**: pick a real problem, gather data, and build a simple model rather than over-planning.
- **Next step after success**: use the pilot's wins and data to fund and scope the next, larger initiative (the flywheel begins).
- **Common trap**: analysis paralysis — endless planning without shipping anything.

## Takeaways
- Pick a small, feasible, valuable pilot and **ship it**.
- A real pilot is the highest-leverage first move in AI transformation.
- Let early wins and data drive the next steps.
