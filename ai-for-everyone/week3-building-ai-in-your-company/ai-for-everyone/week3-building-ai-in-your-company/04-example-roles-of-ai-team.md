# Week 3 — Example Roles of an AI Team

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **3 — Building AI in Your Company** · Lesson: **Example roles of an AI team**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
A mature AI team is more than one "AI person" — it combines engineering, data, product, and leadership roles that work together to ship and operate AI systems.

## Key Points
- **The roles**:
  - **Machine learning engineer** — builds and deploys the learning systems (often alongside the ML researcher / applied ML scientist on harder problems).
  - **Data scientist** — analyzes data and extracts insights that guide decisions.
  - **Data engineer** — builds and maintains the data pipelines that feed models.
  - **AI product manager** — owns the product vision and priorities; the liaison between business and technical teams.
  - **Software engineer** — integrates models into larger products.
  - **AI team lead / manager** — coordinates the team and aligns it with company goals.
- **How the roles interact**: product managers define the A→B task and success metric; engineers build the data + model pipeline; data scientists validate quality; the whole team iterates together.
- **Start lean**: a company can begin with a small "AI group" (a few engineers + a product-minded leader) and grow it as pilots succeed.
- **Reporting structure matters**: an AI team is most effective when it has dedicated resources and a clear mandate, not a side project squeezed into another team's spare time.

## Takeaways
- Understand the distinct roles so you can staff and manage an AI effort properly.
- The **AI product manager** is a key non-coding role for translating business needs.
- Start small (a focused group) and grow as you ship successful pilots.
