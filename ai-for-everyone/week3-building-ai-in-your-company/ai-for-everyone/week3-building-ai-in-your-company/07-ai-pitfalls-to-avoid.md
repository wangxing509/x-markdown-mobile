# Week 3 — AI Pitfalls to Avoid

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **3 — Building AI in Your Company** · Lesson: **AI pitfalls to avoid**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
Most AI initiatives that fail do so because of common, avoidable organizational mistakes — especially unrealistic expectations and treating AI as magic.

## Key Points
- **Pitfall 1 — Expecting AI to solve everything**: AI is a set of narrow tools for specific A→B tasks; expecting it to "do AI" broadly sets up failure. Avoid the "AI or nothing" framing — many valuable projects are incremental, not flashy.
- **Pitfall 2 — Not doing technical diligence**: committing to a project before verifying it is feasible with your data.
- **Pitfall 3 — Under-resourcing the team**: hiring just 2–3 ML engineers and expecting them to do everything — they need data engineers, product managers, and leadership support to succeed.
- **Pitfall 4 — Expecting an AI project to work on the first try**: AI development is **iterative**; treat early pilots as experiments, not guarantees.
- **Pitfall 5 — Expecting traditional planning to work unchanged**: AI projects are harder to schedule precisely because of uncertainty in data and model performance.
- **Pitfall 6 — Treating AI as a black box / magic**: not setting clear success metrics; instead, define measurable targets.
- **Pitfall 7 — Thinking AI is only for tech companies**: every industry (healthcare, retail, agriculture, manufacturing) has AI use cases.
- **General guardrail**: the biggest obstacle is **misaligned expectations** (both hype and fear) — set realistic expectations at the top, invest properly, and tie AI work to measurable business outcomes.

## Takeaways
- Failure is rarely the algorithm's fault — it's usually expectations, resourcing, or scoping.
- Be a demanding-but-realistic sponsor: clear metrics, adequate resources, and domain collaboration.
- Avoid "AI as magic" thinking on both sides (leadership and engineering).
