# Week 3 — Building AI in Your Company: Introduction

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **3 — Building AI in Your Company** · Lesson: **Introduction**
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
Week 3 is the "how to make your organization good at AI" week: it uses detailed case studies, defines an AI team, and lays out a step-by-step **AI Transformation Playbook**.

## Key Points
- **Goal of the week**: give leaders a concrete playbook for embedding AI into a company — not just deploying one tool.
- **Two foundational case studies** teach what complex AI products look like end-to-end:
  - **Smart speaker** → an AI "pipeline" of multiple components.
  - **Self-driving car** → an even more complex pipeline with safety requirements.
- **Key themes**:
  - Building an **in-house AI team** with clear roles.
  - **Broad AI training** across the company, not just for engineers.
  - Developing an **AI strategy** aligned to business goals.
  - Communicating a credible **AI vision** internally and externally.
- **Pragmatic mindset**: AI transformation is an organizational change effort, not a single IT project.

## Takeaways
- AI success in a company = playbook + team + training + strategy + communication.
- The case studies reveal the "pipeline" nature of real AI products.
- Transformation is a **multi-step, organization-wide** journey.
