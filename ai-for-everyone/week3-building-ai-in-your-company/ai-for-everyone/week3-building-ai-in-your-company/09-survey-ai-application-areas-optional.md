# Week 3 — Survey of Major AI Application Areas (optional)

> Course: **AI for Everyone** by Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera)
> Week: **3 — Building AI in Your Company** · Lesson: **Survey of major AI application areas** · *optional*
> Study notes (original summary, not verbatim transcript).

## Core Idea
A broad tour of where AI is applied today, so leaders can recognize which application areas are mature and how they might show up in their own business.

## Key Points
- **Computer vision**:
  - Image classification (what is this?), object detection (where are objects?), and image segmentation (which pixels belong to what).
  - Mature uses: self-driving, medical imaging, manufacturing inspection, face recognition.
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**:
  - Text classification, information retrieval (search), named-entity recognition, and — more recently — **large language models / generative text**.
  - Mature uses: email routing, chatbots, search, translation, summarization.
- **Speech**:
  - Speech recognition (speech → text) and text-to-speech.
  - Mature uses: voice assistants, dictation, call-center transcription.
- **Robotics**:
  - Controlling physical machines; harder than digital AI because of real-world uncertainty.
  - Uses: warehouse robots, drones, industrial automation.
- **Other domains**: recommendation systems, anomaly/fraud detection, forecasting — all built on the same A→B pattern.
- **Practical lens**: almost every business has places where one of these mature application areas can add value.

## Takeaways
- AI application areas cluster into **vision, language, speech, robotics, and structured-data tasks** (recommendation/forecasting).
- Recognize where these are mature enough to use off-the-shelf vs. needing custom work.
- Map your business problems onto the mature application areas.
